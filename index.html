Executive Summary — August 12, 2026
Status: 🟡 AT RISK — 88–93% FKP adoption projected across Commercial, Gia2h, and Blackjack-J. Blackjack-A remains significantly below the 90% FY27 target.

Bottom Line: Blackjack-A migration projections are 20% below the FY27 90% target, driven by org timeline shifts and lengthy cluster-build timelines blocking STP and Security. Confirmed migrations shifting into FY28 (CDP, MonCloud BJ-A, ADX, Thunderbird) have capped maximum achievable FY27 adoption. Two critical timeline gaps remain.

Commercial, Gia2h, & BJ-J: Maximum ceiling is now 88–93%.   BJ-A: Maximum ceiling is now 69.9%.

Key Changes Since Last Report:

  • Projection movement: Commercial and Gia2h Q4 projections increased about 0.5%, while Blackjack-A and Blackjack-J fell 1.3% and 0.3%, respectively.
  • ADX timeline shifts: SFCD Managed Workflows and SFCD Ross Commercial work moved from R266 to R268. SFCD Structured Config and Spinnaker Blackjack work also moved to R268.
  • Blackjack-A cluster delays: More STP teams are formally moving Blackjack-A migrations beyond R266.
  • Einstein Analytics: Moved from On Track to At Risk while the team re-evaluates its approach after recent incidents; clarity is expected in 8b. Flowsnake is consequently also At Risk.

Target: Status is measured against the HPS M6.2.1 90% FKP service-instance target.
