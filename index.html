Executive Summary — July 27, 2026
Status: 🟡 AT RISK — 87–93% FKP adoption projected across Commercial, Gia2h, and Blackjack-J. Blackjack-A remains below target.

Bottom Line: Confirmed migrations shifting into FY28 (CDP, MonCloud BJ-A, RADX, Thunderbird) have capped our maximum achievable FY27 adoption. To hit even these revised ceilings, teams must still close three critical timeline gaps (see Critical Actions Required below).

Commercial, Gia2h, & BJ-J: Maximum ceiling is now 87–93%.   BJ-A: Maximum ceiling is now 71.2%.

Key Changes Since Last Report:

  • Blackjack-A Projection Slip: There was an ~8% slip in FY27 Q4 projections due to STP team shifts (from Blackjack-A cluster build delays), as well as RADX timeline shifts.
  • RADX: Multiple teams shifted their timelines from R266 to R268 — across Commercial (Spinnaker), Gia2h (FIT and PCS Hydration), and Blackjack (PCS, SFCD Managed Workflows, SFCD Multi-Substrate Releases, and TIDE).
  • Thunderbird: Thunderbird FKP Adoption in Commercial will move from FY27 Q3 → FY28 Q1. Team is prioritizing GovCloud enablement based on VA customer FY27 Q4 requirement. FKP leadership is currently aligning on this revised timeline.

Decision: Re-Baseline Update. After discussion and alignment with FKP leadership, the program will re-baseline status reporting against the HPS M6.2.1 90% FKP service instance target. This new target will appropriately reflect FY28 timelines and track against maximum feasible progress.
